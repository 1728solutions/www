My Approach — An Atlas

1728 Solutions is, in a way, an atlas of ways to work with systems—combining modeling, facilitation, and technical stewardship to make complex problems intelligible and actionable.

Core practices

  1. Map: make dependencies and flows visible
  2. Clarify: define interfaces and decision points
  3. Iterate: design small, test, and adapt
  4. Steward: embed practices for lasting alignment

How I engage

Typical engagements begin with a short diagnostic, followed by a time-boxed design and implementation phase with coaching and handoff. I tailor each engagement to the problem's scale and risk profile.
