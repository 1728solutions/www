About 1728 Solutions

Who I am

I work at the intersection of technical systems and organizational effectiveness, helping teams navigate complexity by integrating architecture, modeling, and coaching.

The path here

My work has spanned software development, energy systems, and emerging technologies. Across these domains, I saw the same pattern: technical problems are often entangled with organizational ones, and addressing one without the other leads to limited results.

Why 1728 exists

1728 Solutions is a container for this work—focused on creating clarity and alignment in situations where systems, people, and decisions interact.

The name

1728 (12³) is a reminder that small, well-aligned systems can compound into meaningful outcomes. The work here focuses on identifying those leverage points and designing interventions that hold over time.
